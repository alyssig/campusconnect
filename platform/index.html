Community guidelines

Keeping CampusConnect a place students actually want to use.

§ 01

No slurs or hate speech

In posts, comments, group chats, or usernames. This is checked automatically and reviewed by moderators.

§ 02

No sexual content

CampusConnect is a campus utility, not a dating or adult platform. Keep it about campus life.

§ 03

No harassment or targeted abuse

Disagreements happen. Targeting a specific person repeatedly does not fly here.

§ THE STORY

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of South Carolina building CampusConnect because our campus runs on four or five different apps that don't talk to each other — GroupMe for dorm floors, group chats for clubs, word of mouth for study spots, nothing at all for accessibility issues. I wanted one place students actually own, instead of piecing campus life together from apps that were never built for us specifically.

It started as a single launch page, then grew into a working prototype with a map, a feed, groups, messaging, and a real moderation system — built the same way most real products get built: in public, one feature at a time, with a lot of user feedback along the way.

As head admin, I personally review every appeal — this isn't going to be a platform where a bot decides your account's fate with no way to reach a real person.
