Meet the creator

Hi, I'm Alyssia Gaston.

I'm a Computer Science student passionate about software engineering, game development, robotics, and building technology that solves real-world problems.

I'm currently developing CampusConnect, a platform designed to improve campus life through navigation, accessibility, and student resources — alongside Rebuttal.ai, an AI communication assistant focused on real-time conversational support.

I also write and build in public as mochabytee, documenting my path through computer science with a community of 20,000+ people who care about building useful, memorable things.
